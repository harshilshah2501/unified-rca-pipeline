Interactive walkthrough for one Detect signal on a payment / checkout path. Present topology comes from map/ (Pillar B). Metric and baseline history come from sketch rows under history/ (Pillar A). The Knowledge Graph / map is an at-the-moment view — it does not store time series. Parent page: unified pipeline workflow.

Rooms, not one store. Scope read-joins neighbors from map/ with sketch windows from history/ into a WorkingSet. Do not ask Topology for “what was p99 yesterday,” and do not ask sketch-history to rebuild the whole dependency graph.
