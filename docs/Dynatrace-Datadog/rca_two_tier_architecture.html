SHEET 3 OF 3REV — BRAINSTORMSCOPE: OBSERVE → DETECT, TWO-TIER STORE

Observe → Contextualize → Detect

Phases grouped by objective, not by lifecycle timing. Tier 1 (fast path, Nezha-style) and Tier 2 (structural path, Murphy + Panorama-style) run as separate lanes and only meet inside the Detect Evaluator. The amber arrow at the bottom is where RCA formally begins.
Solid cyan/amber — async push or publish; no back-pressure on the sender.
Dashed grey — synchronous read or blocking dependency; caller waits.
Dotted red — explicitly excluded / off scope.
Amber glow — the RCA hand-off boundary.
Tier 1 — fast path, DragonflyDB, Nezha-style. Tier 2 — structural, Vortex/Arrow + DashMap, Murphy + Panorama-style.
Undecided by design, not by omission: Identity Resolver and Clock Sync are drawn as synchronous, blocking steps in the Tier 1 write path — a record isn't considered stored until both resolve. The alternative (write raw immediately, backfill identity/clock asynchronously) trades completeness for latency. Neither the brainstorm doc nor this diagram settles which one you want; it's flagged here so the choice gets made deliberately.
