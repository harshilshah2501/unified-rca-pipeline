From continuous telemetry on a service chain: what broke, where, what to look at — without re-collecting the estate on every alert.

1) Two layers (do not collapse)

E · Engineering (BTS)

Always-on fabric + case pipeline: Collect → … → Package. Must be able to name a leaf without MTL docs or Bits.

I · Interpret (one run)

Timeline · MTL workbook · Bits review. Explain or package a window; not required storage/Detect engines.

Sticky contract (Datadog hub): Engineering tabs = Hub → Thesis → Breakdown → Three-rail → Timeline. Interpret = MTL workbook · Bits (visually separated).
