Evaluation finding

Aligned with ADR-001 — bakeoff evidence favors lean for classic-infra evaluation; federated remains a valid comparator and hybrid candidate.

Where lean leads

  • Bit-stable reproducibility on UC bakeoff (100% vs ~40%)
  • Auditable tiers / CARE / SRE trail (Verifiability)
  • Respects Roadblock 5 — cheap main path
  • OpenObserve peer: lower multi-trigger title-pick than Fed (behavioral edge)

Where federated / OpenObserve matter

  • Murphy partial structural / counterfactual-style CV
  • Soft ranking via causal posterior over candidates
  • OpenObserve = third RCA method on the 29 (platform narratives)
  • Same 5/5 UC accuracy — CV did not buy extra top-1 on that GT track
